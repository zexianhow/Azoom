About AZoom Car Rental

AZoom Car Rental is a local business focusing purely on electric vehicle hire for environmentally-conscious customers. Enjoy flexible rental plans: daily, weekly or monthly. Book online securely with credit card, collect vehicles at our office branches, and drop them off at our designated branch car parks.
